Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · idAudio (Idan Armoni), HaAvoda 10, Zichron Yaakov, Israel

1. What this is

PickToMic is a downloadable audio plugin. This policy explains what (very little) data is involved when you visit our website, buy the plugin, or run it on your computer.

2. The plugin itself collects nothing

The PickToMic plugin runs entirely on your computer. It does not send any data to us, has no telemetry, no analytics, no “phone home” behaviour, and no online activation check. It writes only two local files inside your user folder:

  • A license file (your serial key) so the plugin remembers it.
  • A small trial-state file (start date) if you use the 14-day trial.

Both stay on your machine. We never see them.

3. The website

This site is static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, cookies, fingerprinting or trackers. GitHub Pages itself may log basic request data (IP, user-agent) for abuse prevention — see GitHub’s privacy policy.

4. When you buy the plugin

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the merchant of record. They receive your name, email and payment information, and they (not us) handle tax, payouts and chargebacks. See Lemon Squeezy’s privacy policy.

From Lemon Squeezy we receive only:

  • Your email address (so we can send your license key).
  • The order ID and product purchased.

We use this to generate and email your license key. We do not sell, share, or use this data for anything else.

5. License delivery email

Your license key is sent through Resend, a transactional email provider. Resend processes the email content (subject, body, your address) only to deliver the message — see Resend’s privacy policy.

6. Your rights (GDPR / Israeli Privacy Law 5741-1981)

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what data we have on you (essentially: your purchase email);
  • delete it (we will, except where retention is required by law for tax records);
  • correct it.

Write to idanarmonilogs@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

PickToMic is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone in that age range.

8. Changes

If we ever change this policy, the “Last updated” date at the top will reflect that. Significant changes will be announced on the homepage.
