Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · idAudio (Idan Armoni)

14-day, no questions asked.
If PickToMic doesn’t work for you for any reason — sound, workflow, system — just email us within 14 days of purchase and we refund. You don’t need to justify it.

1. Why we offer a 14-day window

You get a fully-functional 14-day trial before you buy, so by the time you pay, you should know whether the plugin fits your setup. The 14-day window after purchase is for the edge case where something changes after you pay (a session goes sideways, a host stops loading the plugin, etc.).

2. How to request a refund

Email idanarmonilogs@gmail.com from the address you used to purchase, with:

  • Your order number (from the Lemon Squeezy receipt), or
  • The licence key itself.

Optionally tell us what went wrong — helpful for the next version, but not required.

3. Processing

We confirm within 2 business days. The refund is processed by Lemon Squeezy (the merchant of record) back to your original payment method. Lemon Squeezy typically takes 5–10 business days to settle the funds back to your card or PayPal.

4. What happens to your licence key

Once a refund is issued, your licence key is added to our revocation list. The plugin keeps working until you next reinstall or open it on a different machine, at which point activation will be denied. Trial mode remains available.

5. After the 14-day window

Refunds outside the window are at our discretion. If the plugin fails on your system for a reason that is clearly a defect on our side and we can’t fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund regardless of the date. Cases like “I changed my mind” or “I switched DAWs” six months later, we generally cannot.

6. Chargebacks

Please email us before filing a chargeback. We can almost always resolve the issue (refund, replacement key, technical help) in a day. Chargebacks cost both sides, and Lemon Squeezy may close your account with them, which makes future purchases harder.
