The problem

Pickups never sound like the room.

A piezo or magnetic pickup captures vibration, not air. The result is honky, plasticky, and missing everything that makes an acoustic instrument feel alive on stage or in a mix.

PickToMic

A real mic, every time.

Trained on hundreds of paired pickup & microphone recordings of acoustic instruments, PickToMic replaces the pickup signal with the depth, body, and air of a studio-mic capture — in real time.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is PickToMic, exactly?

A real-time audio plugin that takes the signal from any pickup on an acoustic instrument and reshapes it into the sound of a studio microphone capture. Under the hood it runs a dual IR convolution engine — a Tone IR plus a Body IR — derived from hundreds of paired DI & microphone recordings.

Which instruments are supported?

10 acoustic instruments at launch: Acoustic Guitar, Classical Guitar, Bouzouki, Saz, Oud, Qanun, Cello, Double Bass, Ukulele, and Violin — with 47 curated factory presets. Any acoustic instrument with a pickup will benefit; the presets just give you a calibrated starting point.

How does the 14-day trial work?

Download, install, run. The full plugin works for 14 days with zero feature limits and no audio watermarks. After the trial expires, you can buy a license without reinstalling.

Can I move my license to another computer?

Yes. A single license activates on up to 3 machines, and you can deactivate from any machine at any time. If a computer dies or is lost, just email us and we'll reset that slot for you.

Does it need an internet connection to run?

No. Activation happens once at install — after that PickToMic runs fully offline. Your sessions never call home.

What about latency for live use?

PickToMic uses lightweight IR convolution — latency is well inside what most live monitoring rigs already add. Suitable for in-ear monitors and stage use without noticeable delay.

Is there an AAX (Pro Tools native) build?

Not at launch. PickToMic ships as AU and VST3 on macOS, and VST3 on Windows. AAX is on the roadmap — drop us a line if you need it.

What's the refund policy?

30-day money-back guarantee. If PickToMic doesn't work for you, just write — we refund, no questions asked.
